# MATLAB Engineering Projects

A collection of MATLAB simulations covering structural mechanics, fluid dynamics, and orbital mechanics. Developed as part of an Aerospace Engineering portfolio at Concordia University.

## Projects

### 1. [Beam Deflection Calculator](Beam-Deflection-Calculator/)
Calculates and plots shear force, bending moment, and deflection for three structural beam configurations using Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.

![Beam](Beam-Deflection-Calculator/BeamFigure1.png)

---

### 2. [Projectile Motion with Aerodynamic Drag](Projectile-Drag-Simulation/)
Simulates free-fall with aerodynamic drag for a baseball, skydiver, and feather. Analyzes terminal velocity, Reynolds number, and flow regime classification.

![Drag](Projectile-Drag-Simulation/DragFigure1.png)

---

### 3. [Orbital Mechanics Simulator — Kepler's Laws](Orbital-Mechanics-Simulator/)
Simulates elliptical orbits for ISS, GPS, and Geostationary satellites using Kepler's laws and the vis-viva equation.

![Orbital](Orbital-Mechanics-Simulator/KelperFigure1.png)

---
